🌱 Quit Quest

Count the days since you quit, and collect stickers along the way.

Quit Quest helps you break habits like smoking, vaping or drinking. Each habit counts up from the last time you did it, and you unlock stickers at 1 day, 1 week, 1 month, 1 year and more.

  • Track as many habits as you like
  • Celebrate milestones with stickers and a sticker book
  • Slipped up? Reset without losing your best streak or your stickers
  • Private by design: everything stays on your iPhone
