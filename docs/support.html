Support

Questions, bug reports and feature ideas are welcome.

Contact us: open an issue at github.com/NamiVivi/Habit-Tracker/issues. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

Frequently asked questions

How is a "day" counted?

A day is a full 24 hours from the time you set as your last slip. One month is 30 days.

I slipped up. What happens if I reset?

Your counter starts again from now. Your best streak, your reset count and every sticker you've collected are kept.

I already quit a while ago. Can I start from that date?

Yes. When adding or editing a habit, set Last time to the date and time you stopped. Any stickers you've already earned unlock straight away.

I'm not getting milestone notifications.

Open the iPhone Settings app, go to Notifications → Quit Quest and turn on Allow Notifications.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Your data is stored only on your iPhone. It moves to a new phone if you transfer from an iCloud or computer backup of your old phone.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe left on a habit to delete it, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.
