Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

In short: Quit Quest does not collect, send or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The app stores the habits you add, including their names, icons and start dates, your reset history and the stickers you've earned. This information is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Quit Quest has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. We cannot see your habits or anything else you enter.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders for upcoming milestones directly on your iPhone. They are created and delivered by iOS on your device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

Backups

Like most apps, Quit Quest's data can be included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups. Those backups are managed by you and Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we have no access to them.

Deleting your data

You can delete any habit in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
