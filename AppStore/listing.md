## Version 1.0

### Promotional text
```
Every day counts. Track how long since you last smoked, vaped or drank, and collect a new sticker at every milestone, from your first day to your fifth year.
```

### Description
```
Quit Quest helps you break the habits you're ready to leave behind: smoking, vaping, drinking, sugar, social media, or anything else.

Each habit counts up from the last time you did it, and every milestone unlocks a sticker for your collection.

COUNT EVERY DAY
• Track as many habits as you like
• See days, hours, minutes and seconds since you quit
• A progress ring shows how close you are to your next milestone

EARN STICKERS
• Unlock stickers at 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 15 days, 1 month, 2, 3, 6 and 9 months, 1 year, 2 years and 5 years
• Celebrate each milestone with confetti
• Keep every sticker you earn in your Sticker Book

SLIPS HAPPEN
• Reset a streak in one tap
• Your best streak, reset count and stickers are always kept
• Already quit a while ago? Set your start date in the past and earn the stickers you deserve

STAY MOTIVATED
• Get a notification when you reach your next milestone
• Quick-start presets for common habits

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Everything stays on your iPhone. Quit Quest collects no data.

Quit Quest is a motivational tracker, not medical advice. If you're quitting alcohol or another substance, talk to a doctor about doing it safely.
```

- **Notes:**
```
No account or login is needed. All data is stored on the device; the app has no server.

To see milestone stickers without waiting: tap +, choose a preset (e.g. Smoking), set "Last time" to 10 days ago, and tap Save. A celebration screen appears with the 1 Week sticker; earlier stickers are listed below it and appear in the Sticker Book tab.

Notifications are local only (UNUserNotificationCenter) and fire when the next milestone is reached.
```

## Age rating questionnaire
Answer **None** to everything except:
- **Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References:** Infrequent. The app names habits like smoking and drinking, but it doesn't encourage or show their use.

Apple works out the final rating from your answers.

## Screenshots
- **Required size:** at least one set of 6.9-inch iPhone screenshots, 1290 × 2796 or 1320 × 2868 pixels, portrait. Smaller iPhone sizes are scaled down from these.
- **How:** in Xcode, run the largest iPhone "Pro Max" simulator, set up the screen, then press ⌘S in the Simulator to save a PNG to your Desktop.
- **Suggested set of 5:**
  1. **My Streaks list** with 2–3 habits at different day counts
  2. **Detail screen** with the progress ring and ticking counter
  3. **Celebration screen** for a milestone (e.g. 1 Week ⭐️)
  4. **Sticker Book** with several stickers earned
  5. **New Habit form** showing the quick-start presets
